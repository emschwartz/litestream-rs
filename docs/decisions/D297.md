## D297. Each swarm-reachable plant carries an unaided budget or a stated miss (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5) · test and tooling only (`scripts/planted-bugs.py`, the justfile, the nightly workflow, the entry files)

- **Question:** `just planted-bugs-unaided` printed seeds to the first failure per plant and judged nothing, so a plant the swarm stopped finding went unnoticed. What must a run judge?
- **Choice:** every non-canary entry of `lsr-sim` and `lsr-core` (266 entries, 231 distinct patches) carries `unaided = <seeds>` (the swarm must fail within that many seeds from base 1,000,001) or `unaided_miss = "<why>"`. `just planted-bugs-names` (in `just check`) refuses an entry with neither, with both, or whose entries that share a patch disagree. `--unaided` fails on a budgeted plant found later or never, and on a marked miss the swarm finds within `--seeds` (default 100): the miss is stale and its sentence is false. A plant whose first failure is the clean tree's own is reported as masked and fails too. `just planted-bugs-judge-test` (in `just check`) runs the judge over fake rows.
- **Budget rule, fixed before the judging run:** budget = min(300, max(4 x measured, 50)), where measured is the seeds to first failure in the measuring run. The cap is that run's seed count, so a plant first found past seed 75 gets 300, which is headroom of under 4x.
- **Measuring run, registration 2 (the one that sets the budgets):** `--unaided` with 300 seeds per plant from base 1,000,001 at 2426bde83 (main at f14080b7e, after the queued sim changes of #502), over all 231 patches: 47 failed within 300 seeds and 184 did not. Measured seeds to first failure, sorted: 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6, 7, 8, 8, 13, 17, 18, 18, 18, 21, 26, 29, 29, 29, 30, 33, 37, 44, 49, 73, 76, 79, 86, 94, 116, 139, 139, 139, 151, 204, 206, 211, 211. 18 plants get the floor of 50 and 13 the cap of 300; the budgets sum to 6,736 seeds. The generator was not changed by this work; registration 1 (the same run at 4918e715e, before #502) is discarded because #502 changed the swarm: of its 46 found plants, three were not found at the new head (identity-check-in-one-tick, maintenance-another-writer, refused-compaction-keeps-uploads-healthy) and seven of its misses were (asof-straddle, level-jumps-to-the-newest-input, mark-zero-empty-generation, short-pass-plans-past-a-hole, slice-that-sent-takes-no-turn, snapshot-runs-whole, time-provenance). Recall moved with an unrelated sim change in both directions, which is the point of budgets: only the budget notices.
- **Judging run:** the same deterministic base (1,000,001), at the head that lands. The swarm is deterministic per seed, so this is a regression gate (it fails when a swarm change, a generator change or a patch drift moves a first failure past its budget), not a fresh estimate of recall. A fresh base would measure recall again, but 4x headroom would then flap on seeds the measuring run never saw; recall on a second base is a separate registration. A miss is judged on its first 100 seeds only: the 300-seed run found nothing in them, so a failure there means the swarm changed.
- **Finding, the gate fires:** a first judging run at 6568df761 (the budgets of registration 1, main at f6c379c5d, nine commits later) judged 255 of 259 entries as recorded and failed four: boot-gate-refuses-nothing (first failure at seed 249, budget 300) and clock-step-back-freezes-timers (seed 4, budget 50), each with its `-sim` entry, were not found in their budget. #495 (a retry's wait timed from when the failed call returned) is the likeliest cause for the clock plant, and nothing was bisected. A plant found at seed 4 and lost to eight commits is one whose reach depends on a draw, not on a generator that reaches the state on purpose.
- **Misses:** 204 entries (184 patches) carry `unaided_miss`, one sentence each over ten sentences of seven classes, plus a specific sentence for each of the 19 plants added or lost since the first pass. A sentence is a classification read from the entry's own comment and its named tests, not a trace of why the seeds pass: the stale check keeps it from drifting one way, and a sentence proved wrong by a trace is rewritten. The classes: fail-stop lag that only a named fail-stop scenario holds long enough; cost plants (requests, memory, bytes) that change no restored page; compaction or listing state that needs a backlog or a hole; the local course; single decision points needing one precise fault; plants in the simulator's own judges; interleavings at one request or tick.
- **Cost, measured:** 500 seeds of the clean tree took 290 s (0.58 s a seed at load average 14); a 300-seed plant run with its build took 100 to 125 s. Registration 2 took about four hours of wall time on three shards of a loaded machine (load 10 to 55); the first judging run took about one hour on three shards at 100-seed miss runs. Budgeted plants run their budget (6,736 seeds in total), misses run 100 (18,400), and each shard runs the clean tree once for up to 300: about 27,500 seeds and 231 builds, estimated at 25 to 30 minutes a shard in eight shards (about 220 runner-minutes a night, ubuntu-latest). The nightly job `planted-bugs-unaided` has a 60-minute limit; the first nightly calibrates the estimate. Misses at 300 seeds would cost about 55,000 seeds, over 60 minutes a shard.
- **Also changed:** the swarm runs 25 seeds per nextest invocation (was 100): at 100 a loaded box passed the 90 s per-test limit, which showed as `ERROR` with no first-failure line (24 of 219 plants in the first pass).
- **Cost accepted:** a budget with 4x headroom detects a swarm that got much worse, not one that got somewhat worse (a first failure moving from 6 to 40 passes), and a plant found by one lucky seed (like those at 204, 211 or 249) flaps with any change to the swarm's draw: every sim change can move the budgets, and each one is then re-measured in the same commit. The miss sentences are hypotheses until a generator change tests them.
- **Revisit if:** the generator changes (re-measure and rewrite every budget in one commit), a shard nears 40 minutes, or the stale-miss check fires (set the budget from that run).
