## D241. A snapshot that had to wait for a sync is taken from the same decision (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5) · Recorded after the other decisions of this change

Found by counting requests in lsr-core on the short-interval scenario of the head-to-head against Go: 21 whole listings (one request per level, 210 in all) in 330 seconds, for three snapshots.

- **Question:** a snapshot needs a proof of the database at a position the replica holds. Its step first syncs, and a writer that commits every tick leaves commits to stage, which ends the tick: they upload, and the next maintenance tick listed the whole replica and planned the snapshot again, to find the same decision and sync again. How many listings may one snapshot cost?
- **What Go does.** It takes a snapshot from the database with no listing and no wait for uploads (`db.go`, `Snapshot`).
- **Choice.** When the snapshot's step staged files, the engine keeps the snapshot job in memory and the next maintenance tick takes the snapshot again from it, with no listing. A failed or interrupted attempt does not keep it, and a restart does not know it: the next pass lists as before. Nothing is written to disk.
- **Cost accepted.** The retry runs on a decision up to a few ticks old. What could change in those ticks is another writer's snapshot, which the next whole listing finds.
- **Counted.** `actor::tests::steady::a_steady_writer_with_short_intervals_pays_the_bill_and_no_more`: whole listings in 330 seconds, 21 before, 6 after (level 5 is listed only by a whole listing).
- **Links:** `crates/core/src/actor.rs` (`snapshot_job`, `maintain_unit`), D238.
