## D209. A restore bounds its whole listing phase by one deadline (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5)

- **Question:** a restore against an endpoint that accepts connections and never answers took about 33 minutes to fail: each level's listing was retried five times, each try waiting out the 60 s request deadline, levels in turn. A person runs a restore in the middle of a recovery. What bounds the listing phase?
- **Go:** retries nothing in the restore's listing (`ReplicaClient.LTXFiles` returns the first error), so a silent endpoint fails at the SDK's own request timeout. Any bound here is no worse than Go.
- **Choice:** the restore's store is a `Retrying` built with `with_listing_budget`: 120 seconds from the first listing call (`RESTORE_LISTING_BUDGET`). A failed listing whose next retry would begin at or after that time is returned as it came, and the CLI replaces the error with one that names the endpoint, the seconds, the listing requests made and the last failure. Only listings are bounded; reads, deletes and replication keep the policy alone, and the daemon's restore-if-missing keeps it too.
- **Cost accepted:** (1) The bound is 120 s plus one request deadline, because a request in flight is not cancelled; a hung request is cut by the transport, not by the budget. (2) A live but slow endpoint whose listings take longer than 120 s in total fails the restore instead of finishing; the retry is the operator's. (3) The number is a constant, not a flag: a new flag is user-visible vocabulary and waits for the owner. (4) The error text is new user-visible wording and waits for the owner's approval.
- **Options considered:** a per-request deadline for restores (does not bound levels in series); a wrapper store outside the retry layer (cannot stop the retries inside, so the bound would be the budget plus a full retry sequence); cancelling the whole restore after a time (would cut a long download).
- **Revisit if:** a legitimate restore from a distant or throttled endpoint trips the budget: raise the constant, or make it a flag.
- **Links:** `crates/core/src/retry.rs` (`with_listing_budget`), `crates/cli/src/replica.rs`, `docs/BEHAVIOR.md` REST-17.
