## D317. A regression holds its degraded cause and doubling wait until a listing shows the replica whole (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: delegated, Claude (claude-opus-5-5), 2026-11-28, for the owner; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5)

- **Question:** a store whose every listing leaves out the newest file had the engine take its own upload as lost, send it again and list again, at about 1,400 LIST/s behind an empty `degraded` list ([#540](https://github.com/emschwartz/litestream-rs/issues/540)). Lagging listings are unsupported (D173), but AGENTS.md caps every retry and reports a fault that persists. What bounds it, and what does health say?
- **Cause:** two loops. Each maintenance listing ended below what the engine confirmed and raised `remote_regressed`, whose relist and re-send succeeded, and that success cleared the cause and reset the wait to its first second, so the next round started from scratch. And once the staged copy of the hidden id had been replaced by a snapshot, the create-only put was refused, the read-back found other bytes, and the relist, still hiding the key, planned the same key again: refusal, read-back and relist repeated inside one tick with no wait at all.
- **Decision:** `remote_regressed` outlasts an upload's success, with its failure count, and ends only when a maintenance listing, taken while every staged file is confirmed, does not end below what the engine confirmed. Each regression seen before that waits twice the last, through the existing doubling (`retry_wait`, capped at `MAX_BACKOFF`, 5 minutes). An upload refused as taken whose relist still ends below the refused key is a regression (`Fault::RemoteRegressed`), routed and waited, never a second put in the same tick. No new cause name: the listing ends below a file the replica holds, which is what `remote_regressed` says.
- **Same loop, supported case:** something that keeps deleting each new file (docs/BEHAVIOR.md row UP-11) went round the same first loop; the same wait and signal bound it.
- **Cost accepted:** while the cause stands, the upload task waits out each doubled wait, so new commits reach the replica up to 5 minutes late, and after the fault ends the engine notices within one wait (up to 5 minutes) rather than at once. Restores stay exact; the replica was already not advancing in this state. A single rollback, the common case, is unchanged: the next maintenance listing after the re-send shows the replica whole and clears the cause.
- **Measured (core actor test, a commit every 500 ms):** in the fault's second minute, at most 12 uploads and 600 LIST requests (about 5 a second, from maintenance's own short pass); before the fix the test went past its cap of 5,000 store requests inside the first minute of the fault and failed.
- **Revisit if:** an operator needs new commits to keep flowing through a persistent regression (a wait that holds only the re-check, not the uploads), or the simulator models lagging listings ([#469](https://github.com/emschwartz/litestream-rs/issues/469)).
