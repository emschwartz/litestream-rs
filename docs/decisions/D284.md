## D284. After the last boot, a tick that waits for a part counts as a slice, not as a settle tick (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5) · test code only (`crates/sim`)

- **Question:** at the end of every run the simulator starts the engine again and gives it 200 ticks to become idle (`SETTLE_TICKS`); ticks that send a slice of a staged file are counted apart, against the drain's cap of 10,000. A large-profile seed (`0x0000126d69302c73`, 5,000 steps, `LSR_SIM_LARGE=1`) failed there with "ticks to drain: 1 is over the limit 0". Is the engine stuck, or is the budget wrong?
- **What happened in the seed:** refusals from the replica (`Denied`, a missing bucket) outlasted the drain's 10-second grace, so 26 staged files of about 11 KB each were still owed when the engine started again. The S3 replica sent them in parts, a part per slice. Between slices the engine answered `Idle` while its upload waited for a part to land, which is what a real driver sees too: the store, not the engine's wake, says when to go on. Those waits were counted as settle ticks, and with 164 of them the 200 ran out while every file was still moving. The engine was making progress on every tick; nothing about it was wrong.
- **Choice:** in that loop, a tick whose event is `Idle` while `Actor::awaits_parts` holds counts as a slice. The settle budget then measures ticks that send nothing and wait on nothing, and the sending as a whole stays bounded by the drain's cap.
- **Evidence:** `parts::scenarios::ticks_waiting_for_parts_after_the_last_boot_count_as_slices` (48 large commits staged under revoked credentials, three refusals that outlast the grace, 64-byte parts on one lane) fails on the old loop with the seed's message and passes now; the seed replays clean.
- **Cost accepted:** an engine that waits on parts forever after the last boot is caught at 10,000 ticks rather than 200. A part lands at the engine's next question in the simulator, so such a wait is a store that never answers, which the drain's cap and the stall checks already judge.
- **Revisit if:** a backlog after the last boot can hold more than 200 files: each file's completing `Uploaded` tick still counts as a settle tick.
