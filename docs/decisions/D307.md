## D307. The fail-stop judge no longer excuses a trip after a boot that broke continuity (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5) · Retires the third amendment of D081

- **Question:** D081's third amendment excused an age trip after a boot whose logged cause says continuity broke (`StagedUnverified`, `LocalRewound`, `AheadOfReplica`), until the engine's first upload since. Its premise was that the boot deletes staged files the live WAL cannot vouch for and snapshots their end state. Does the premise still hold?
- **Choice:** no, so the exemption is gone. Commit 1edc0905b (#285) removed the bucket-rollback machinery, and the boot no longer deletes staged files the WAL cannot vouch for (`crates/sim/src/reach.rs` says so). The snapshot holds only commits the WAL and staging hold, and the engine counts it as lag by what it can know. The judge's `unvouched_snapshot` count, its tick field and its reach key `failstop.excused.unvouched_snapshot` are removed. A trip after such a boot is judged like any other: it needs a failed attempt at or after the bound's instant.
- **Cost accepted:** none known. If a seed finds a false trip here, the engine or the judge is wrong and the run fails, which is the point.
- **Revisit if:** a boot gains a way to hold commits the engine never staged and cannot compare.
- **Links:** D081, D282; issue #437; `crates/sim/src/failstop.rs`.
