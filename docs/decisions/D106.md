## D106. An ahead-of-WAL check the replica cannot answer (delegated)

Status: open to owner review · Decided by: Claude (journal), **delegated**, decided by a Fable review; names owner-approved

- **Question:** D103's check compares the database file with the replica's state at its newest confirmed transaction. When the replica cannot give that state because it is damaged (simulator seed 0x2dc719: a bucket rollback took a level-0 file, the engine uploaded past the hole, and a restore of the confirmed state cannot plan across it), the check failed `RemoteCorrupt` and retried every tick for ever. The TRUNCATE that pays the debt never ran, the WAL grew, the unsynced frames stayed exposed, and no snapshot healed the gap. What should the check do?
- **Choice:** tell damage from a failed call, and give the check up when damage recurs. A failed read or listing page, or a replica changing under the restore (`Unstable`, `ListingBehind`), is a store fault, retried as any is. A history that does not plan, a file or database that does not verify, a store `Corrupt`, or a listing no real store returns is damage. The first attempt that finds damage degrades the sync task at `RemoteCorrupt` and waits its backoff, since one flipped read or a listing that hid a file looks like damage once. The next attempt that finds it gives the check up. The notice is held and journalled (record version 3), and the sync task is degraded at `DatabaseAheadOfWalUnchecked` through `Responses::database_ahead_of_wal`, so `STRICT` stops before the TRUNCATE and the default runs the cure. The notice is `Health.ahead_of_wal_unchecked`, `/healthz` `database_ahead_of_wal_unchecked` (`since`, `kept_until`), `lsr_database_ahead_of_wal_unchecked` and an error-level log line, plus `Event::AheadOfWal` (`UncheckedStands` at a boot inside the window, `UncheckedEnded` at its end). It stands seven days, as D103's does, and a later one restarts the window. No restore point is kept: the state it would keep is the one the replica cannot give. The check's restore plans once a tick (`restore_one_plan`): the actor retries on a later tick itself, and three plans in one tick spent three listings of every level. The simulator expects `RemoteCorrupt` and `DatabaseAheadOfWalUnchecked` after a bucket rollback that removed files.
- **Options considered:** report it as D103's verdict with no pages (claims a mix nobody proved); check only the pages the page table covers and give up on the rest (a partial verdict pins a restore point that cannot be restored, and still needs this for the rest); keep retrying (the liveness failure); find the images elsewhere (nothing local holds them below the confirmed transaction); give up at the first attempt (one flipped read or a lagging listing would destroy the evidence for good, since the TRUNCATE follows).
- **Cost accepted:** a database file that may be ahead of its WAL is replicated as a clean commit, marked only by the notice, which a lost or unwritable journal forgets. One more full replica read for the confirming attempt. A crash between giving up and the TRUNCATE makes the next boot check again. The notice carries no cause: it has one, and the log of the first attempt names the restore's error. A replica that is unreachable rather than damaged still holds the check, as D103 accepted.
- **Revisit if:** unchecked boots are seen outside the simulator; the WAL's extent at the failure is journalled (D103's own revisit), which removes the replica read; a partial check against the page table proves cheap and useful.
- **Names:** owner-approved (2026-11-23): `Health.ahead_of_wal_unchecked`, `PersistentCause::DatabaseAheadOfWalUnchecked` (and `StopCause`), `lsr_replica::AheadOfWalUnchecked`, `Actor::ahead_of_wal_unchecked`, `/healthz` `database_ahead_of_wal_unchecked` with `since` and `kept_until`, and `lsr_database_ahead_of_wal_unchecked`, each its D103 sibling with `_unchecked` added.
