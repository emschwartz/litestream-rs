## D206. The boot's sweep of open uploads is best effort (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5)

- **Question:** The first thing a boot does is remove the scratch files and abort the open multipart uploads a killed process left (`remove_scratch`). On S3 that is ListMultipartUploads and one AbortMultipartUpload per upload. The policy Litestream's S3 guide publishes (GetBucketLocation, ListBucket, PutObject, DeleteObject, GetObject) allows neither, so a 403 there was classified as a refusal at boot and the boot never reached its listing: under `hold-start` the application's writes stayed held until someone changed IAM. Reproduced on MinIO with a user holding exactly those five actions. What should a refusal of the sweep do?
- **What Go does:** nothing. Go sweeps no uploads and needs neither permission.
- **Choice:** A refusal for want of permission (`Denied::Permission`) of the replica's sweep is skipped: the boot goes on to its listing, the process does not ask again, and the skip is reported once (a warning log, `Event::ScratchSweepDenied`, `lsr_scratch_sweeps_denied_total`). Any other failure of the sweep (a timeout, a refusal of the credentials, the clock or the bucket) fails the boot attempt as before: it says the replica is not answering, and the listing would meet it too. The local staging sweep stays strict: a failure there is the directory's, which every upload needs. If the listing's own permission is missing, the listing fails the same way and holds the start as it should.
- **Cost accepted:** where the two actions are not granted, uploads a killed process left open stay open and are billed until a bucket `AbortIncompleteMultipartUpload` rule or an operator removes them. `docs/s3-permissions.md` says so and names the rule.
- **Options considered:** skip every refusal class (the credential, clock and bucket refusals would then surface one request later at the listing with the same hold, so nothing is gained and the skip would say less); retry the sweep at every boot (a request and a log line per attempt for an answer that does not change until IAM does, and a restart picks a fixed policy up anyway).
- **Revisit if:** a store reports the refusal under another class, or open uploads left by crash loops become a cost people report.
- **Links:** `docs/s3-permissions.md`, `docs/decisions/D192.md`, `crates/core/src/actor.rs` (`remove_scratch`).
