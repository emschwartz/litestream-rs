## D163. Long-lived tests state their behaviour; seeds find bugs and never pin them

Status: decided · Decided by: owner, 2026-11-23 · Recorded by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5)

- **Question:** a regression the simulator found was pinned as a seed number or as a recorded history of up to 300 operations, guarded by a planted bug in the ledger (`docs/planted-bugs/`). Every engine or generator change moved some of those histories off their defect, so a pinned test passed for nothing until the ledger noticed; in one day seven entries survived their plants after one batch of merges, three tests went red for that reason alone, and the repairs were further recordings that would decay the same way. Keep pinning seeds, or state each regression explicitly?
- **Choice:** a long-lived test states its behaviour explicitly. Random seeds are for finding bugs, never for pinning them. A regression test is a short explicit scenario that names the circumstance and the expected outcome, never a seed number or a recorded run.
- **Consequences accepted:**
  1. The planted-bug ledger is retired after one final full run that confirms every named test catches its bug: the patches, `scripts/planted-bugs.py` and its gate go. The checker plants and the canaries become ordinary tests that hand the checker a bad run.
  2. Planted-bug entries that rely only on seed pins are converted into direct tests before that final run.
  3. The recorded histories under `crates/sim/tests/sim/histories/` are converted into short explicit scenarios, or deleted where a direct test already covers the bug. A recording stays only while nobody can yet say why it fails.
  4. The behaviour table (`docs/behavior.toml`) stops citing seed-numbered tests.
  5. The simulator runs fresh seeds only: the nightly soak and the campaigns search, and what they find becomes an explicit scenario.
  6. The generator's default mix is unfrozen once nothing pins its draws: a reason that existed only so recorded histories kept their draws no longer holds.
- **Cost if wrong:** an explicit scenario covers the circumstance its author understood, not every interleaving the seed happened to take. A defect that only a long random run reaches is found again by the soak, not kept by a test, until someone can state it.
