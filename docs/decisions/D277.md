## D277. The nightly runs a large profile: 5,000-step histories, 8 times the commit size, four shards (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5) · test and CI code only (`crates/sim`, `justfile`, `.github/workflows/sim-nightly.yml`)

- **Question:** the nightly runs 300-step seeds. A bug that needs a long history (many generations, compaction levels 3 and up, retention over many snapshots, WAL growth across many checkpoints, a snapshot spanning many slices) is under-sampled there. What profile samples it, and what does it cost?
- **Choice, the profile** (`Weights::mixed(seed).with_nightly_laws(seed).with_large_sizes()`, test `soak::long_seeds`):
  - 5,000 steps a seed (`LONG_STEPS`), 17 times the rotating set's.
  - Every commit writes 8 times the pages (`LARGE_SIZE_SCALE`, 8 to 40 pages instead of 1 to 5), and a commit grows the database half the time instead of a sixth. The draws are the same as at scale 1, so the history keeps its shape (`generate::tests`).
  - No page-size change (`vacuum_resizes` off): after one the engine is degraded for the rest of the run, which a long history spends nearly all of (12,747 degraded ticks over 7 seeds before this was turned off, none after).
  - The nightly's laws stay on.
- **Choice, the seeds:** the upper half of the night's million (`long_base`: `nightly_base` plus 500,000), so no seed is shared with the rotating set, which counts up from `nightly_base` and runs thousands a night. `LSR_SIM_SHARD=i/n` gives shard i the seeds at offsets i, i+n, i+2n from the base; shards are disjoint and cover the range (`seed::tests`).
- **Choice, the CI:** a matrix job of 4 shards, 1,200 s of seeds each (`just sim-long <seconds> <day> <i>/<n>`), each uploading `sim-reach-long-<job index>`. A replay line is `LSR_SIM_LARGE=1 LSR_SIM_NIGHTLY_LAWS=1 just sim-replay 0x<seed> 5000`.
- **Measured** on the development machine (Apple silicon, the `sim` profile, shared with other jobs): 14 seeds over 7 + 7 across two shards took 129 s and 137 s of wall time for 90 s and 120 s budgets, about 19 s a seed. One shard therefore runs about 63 seeds in 1,200 s, 250 seeds (1.25 million steps) a night over the four. Seven seeds already put 72 level-3 files, 55 snapshots and 7,708 level-0 files on the replica. Expected CI time: 4 jobs of about 8 minutes of build and 20 minutes of seeds, about 115 job-minutes a night; the job's timeout is 40 minutes.
- **Not gated:** the marks gate needs every row reached, which a shard of tens of seeds cannot promise, and the rotating set already gates the rows with the coverage scenarios. The long set only fails on a failing seed; its reach files are written for comparison and can be summed by hand.
- **Cost accepted:** about 115 more job-minutes a night. A seed that grows the database stays small beside a real one (page numbers a commit picks stop at 256), so the many-slice snapshot is reached through the slice settings of the swarm mix, not through database size.
- **Revisit if:** a night's seeds per shard fall far below 60 (the runner is slower than assumed), a bug of this class is found by neither profile, or the generator draws sizes of its own.
