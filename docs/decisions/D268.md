## D268. The healthy tail bounds the WAL at Go's PASSIVE minimum (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5) · test code only (`crates/sim`)

- **Question:** the simulator recorded the WAL's largest frame count but judged it only in the outage scenario. An engine that stopped running PASSIVE after a sync at the minimum passed every seed, because the 60 s interval and the truncate threshold still emptied the WAL later. What bound should the WAL meet?
- **What Go does, and why:** after a sync that leaves at least `MinCheckpointPageN` synced frames, Go checkpoints PASSIVE (`db.go` `checkpointIfNeeded`). With no reader holding a snapshot, the next write restarts the WAL from its start.
- **Choice:** in the healthy tail (`crates/sim/src/run/tail.rs`, D263), each time a tail commit reaches the replica with no fault in its window and no other-process reader open, the WAL holds at most the simulator's PASSIVE minimum (8), the commit's 2 frames and the engine's bump frame: 11 (`wal_bound`). `wal_bound.judged` is a required mark.
- **Margin:** the largest seen over the whole `sim` suite is 9. The plant `checkpoint-skips-the-minimum-after-sync` reaches 15.
- **Cost accepted:** a commit whose window saw a fault, or one made with a reader open, is not judged (`wal_bound.excused`). The application's own long readers get their own scenario.
- **Revisit if:** the tail's commits change size, or the simulator's PASSIVE minimum changes.
