## D112. The closed-world matrix the soak is gated on (delegated)

Decided by: **delegated**

- **Options:** keep gating the soak on fault classes only; gate it on every cell of the error domain (operation by error, for SQLite and both stores) and every app action point (engine call by app action); add a law that kills the process right after a fault.
- **Choice:** the cells and the points. Each cell has `cell.<seam>.<Op>.<Class>.drawn` and `.hit` keys (`crates/sim/src/domain/marks.rs`): a drawn or known-wrong cell must be hit over a night, an impossible or unmodelled one must stay undrawn. Each point has `point.<SourceOp>.<Action>.ran` (`crates/sim/src/domain/points.rs`), all drawn. The older error subset the default mix draws from passes every draw through the table (`possible_source`, `possible_store`), so it no longer injects errors the shipped implementations cannot return (busy on calls that take no lock, throttling from a filesystem); its entropy is spent as before, so its other draws keep their values. The nightly alone (`Weights::with_nightly_laws`) gives half its seeds a TRUNCATE threshold of 2 to 8 frames with other processes' readers at weight 10 or more, so the engine's own TRUNCATE and the snapshot under the write lock are reached; no mix draws it, so no pinned seed moves. The kill after a fault is the journal session's, under #171 item 5.
- **Why:** a class count can be high while most of a table's drawn cells never fire (#171 item 2). The first soak with cell counts found the older subset injecting impossible errors and the engine's TRUNCATE in 64 of 2860 seeds.
- **Cost accepted:** a cell or point a night misses makes it red until an exemption with its measured reason says otherwise; the write-lock cells and points stay rare even with the law (over 3063 seeds: TRUNCATE in 152, `BeginWriteLock.DiskFull` hit 4 times, `EndWriteLock.Io` once), and the page-count points rarely run because an app step arms for one tick. Each such key is in `EXEMPT` and turns stale when reached.
- **Revisit if:** the write-lock cells need real coverage: an injection armed to wait for the engine's next TRUNCATE, or a profile that truncates every run.
- **Links:** #171, #127, `docs/behavior.toml` BOOT-18.
