## D256. Every app action point is reached by a coverage scenario

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5), delegated · Recorded by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5)

- **Question:** `just sim-nightly 180 20261125` fails its coverage gate with no failing seed. Over about 1,800 seeds, `point.PageCount.Spill.ran` and `point.BumpSeq.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` stay at zero. A second run of 1,393 seeds left `point.BeginBarrier.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` at zero instead. The first mark became required when the app's open write transaction (`AppOp::Spill`) joined the in-tick actions. That action added a page-count point as rare as its exempt siblings and thinned every other action's share.
- **Measured (the nightly soaks of 24 and 25 November 2026, 23,017 seeds):** the rarest app action points ran about 0.1 times per 1,000 seeds, and about twenty more ran 1 to 3 times per 1,000. The rarest error cell ran 18 times per 1,000, the rarest fault-class count 40, and the rarest app or operator step count over 66.
- **Choice:** the low rate is a property of the whole family, not of a few marks. A point needs the app's action armed in the one tick that makes the engine's call. So `crates/sim/src/scenario/coverage.rs` now builds one scenario for every drawn point, from a situation picked by an exhaustive match on the call. That makes 80 scenarios, 67 of them new, and it drops the 8 point exemptions, whose marks are now required and reached. Error cells, fault classes and steps stay with the seeds, each family with its reason in `LEFT_TO_THE_SEEDS`. `marks::every_required_mark_has_a_scenario_or_a_reason` holds every required key to exactly one of the two.
- **Why:** covering marks one by one, as they fail, leaves the next rare point to luck. Exempting them would require less of the soak. The scenarios cost well under a second together.
- **Cost accepted:** the gate no longer shows that random histories reach any app action point, and a generator change that stops drawing them passes it. The rates above, and each night's uploaded reach file (whose point counts now include the scenarios' one each), are the record of what random draws reach.
