## D256. Two more coverage scenarios: the PASSIVE before the sequence bump, the write lock before the page count

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5), delegated · Recorded by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5)

- **Question:** `just sim-nightly 180 20261125` fails its coverage gate with no failing seed: `point.PageCount.Spill.ran` and `point.BumpSeq.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` stay at zero over about 1,800 seeds. The first became required when the app's open write transaction (`AppOp::Spill`) joined the in-tick actions, which added a page-count point as rare as its exempt siblings. The second was already rare, and an eighth action thinned every action's share.
- **Measured (two nightly reach files, the nightly's laws):** `point.PageCount.Spill.ran` 1 in 7,046 and 1 in 11,331 seeds; `point.BumpSeq.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` 2 in 7,046 and 5 in 11,331 (10 in 11,686 before the new action).
- **Choice:** each gets a scenario in `crates/sim/src/scenario/coverage.rs`, as D182 does for the other rare points, and the gate still requires both. `marks::every_required_page_count_point_has_a_coverage_scenario` fails the build for the next page-count point a new action adds without a scenario.
- **Why:** exempting `PageCount.Spill` beside the other page-count points would have required less of the soak; a scenario costs milliseconds and keeps the point checked.
- **Not covered:** a soak much shorter than the nightly's 900 seconds still fails by chance. At 180 seconds on a loaded machine (1,400 to 1,800 seeds) about twenty required points that run 1 to 3 times per 1,000 seeds can each come up empty; one run left `point.BeginBarrier.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` at zero. The 900-second nightly runs 7,000 to 11,000 seeds and reaches them all. Covering them too would make the gate prove nothing about what random histories reach, so a short soak that fails only its gate, with no failing seed, is not by itself a finding.
- **Cost accepted:** as in D182, these two marks are no longer evidence that random histories reach them; the rates above are that record.
- **Links:** D182; `crates/sim/src/marks.rs`.
