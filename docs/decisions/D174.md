## D174. A degraded state does not have to survive a restart

Status: decided · Decided by: owner, 2026-11-23 · Recorded by: Claude (claude-fable-5-2)

- **Question:** D173 left open whether retry counts, waits and degraded states must survive a restart. Each run counts from zero: a retried fault becomes a degraded state after eight failures in a row in one process. A replicator that restarts sooner than that (a crash loop, a supervisor that restarts it on an unhealthy report) can halt replication for any length of time while health reports nothing. The simulator counts this as an open defect (`HaltForgottenAtRestart`: 136 of the 143 failing seeds in the soak of 2026-11-23 on 8e10a9bb). Go forgets the same things.
- **Choice (owner):** leave it out of v1, for now. A restart forgets retry counts, waits and degraded states, as Go does. It is listed as not covered in D173's "Left out" table and in `docs/differences-from-go.md`.
- **What still carries over:** the age of a commit that was staged and never uploaded (`unreplicated_since` and `lsr_unreplicated_age_seconds`, dated from the staged file; replica test `a_restart_keeps_the_age_of_a_commit_off_the_replica`), and the `OtherWriter` stop, because every boot refuses the other writer's files again.
- **Cost accepted:** a fault that stops anything from being staged (a full local disk) leaves no staged file to date, so through a crash loop neither the degraded state nor the age reports it. An operator who needs that alarm watches the supervisor's restart count beside `lsr_unreplicated_age_seconds`.
- **What changes:** the simulator's law for a halt forgotten at a restart and its known-defect entry go, in a change of their own after the bucket-rollback removal lands (both touch the simulator's run loop). The open-defect row D9 of `docs/BEHAVIOR.md` becomes an accepted limit. The rest of issue #99 is unaffected.
- **Why:** remembering across a restart needs saved state, and D173's change 4 removes the only place the engine saves any (the journal). D173's rule gives the default: what Go does, said loudly where a check with no remembered state can see it. Within one run the degraded state is that loud report.
- **Revisit if:** a real deployment reports replication halted through restarts with health quiet, or a way to carry the state appears that adds no remembered state of its own (the staged file's date is the model).
- **Links:** D173, D80, D85; BEHAVIOR D9 and N1; issues #99, #115.
