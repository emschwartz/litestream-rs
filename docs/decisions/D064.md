## D64. The read budget charges each probe what the stores allow

Status: open to owner review · Decided by: Claude, **delegated** (route from the parent session) · 2026-11-23

- **The case:** boots that fail one after another on local faults (staging reads, the WAL) list the replica and probe past it each time, through everything staged past the listing, or to the walk's cap with nothing staged: about 190 reads apiece in one random simulator run, 140 in another. The budget once charged each probe 12 reads, the cost of its gap window, and called both runs a storm. Main then charged every boot and fault `WALK_READS_MAX` (320), which passed them with 7 to 15 times headroom, so a real storm of that size would pass too.
- **Options:** keep a boot's listing across ticks so a retry after a local fault skips the probe (a cache, declined); charge the worst case per boot and fault (main before this); charge per listing what the stores around it allow.
- **Choice:** per listing. The recorder's listing calls over the level count, rounded up, count the probes a tick made. Each looks for at most the newest transaction staging or the replica holds plus the gap, capped, or the cap with nothing staged; it names 4 keys per transaction, plus 2 per level 1 to 8 ever written inside its gap window. Those are the probe's own documented naming rules, recomputed from the world, not read from the engine. Retries stay bounded by the backoff and the liveness checks, not by this budget.
- **Cost accepted:** headroom of about 2 to 4 on the runs that walk most, so an engine that walks twice per listing on those runs passes. The reach counts from transaction 1, as the oracle cannot see where a lagging listing ends, so runs past 64 transactions charge every probe its cap.
- **Evidence:** explicit cases in `crates/sim/src/budget.rs` (no recorded runs, per D165): a listing of every level is one probe; nothing staged charges the cap; a backlog charges through itself and the gap, never past the cap; repeated boots inside their listings' charge pass, and twice that fails.
- **Revisit if:** a run shows reads the per-listing charge misses (a probe without a listing), or the oracle learns the listing's end.
- **Links:** `crates/sim/src/budget.rs` (`Walked`), `crates/core/src/probe.rs` (`walk`), D63.
