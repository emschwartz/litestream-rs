## D303. The witness token's byte check is the CRC-64 `Seen`, not a hand-written lane hash (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-opus-5-5) · Supersedes: D210

- **Question:** two types did one job. `ByteTally` (`crates/core/src/witness.rs`, a hand-written four-lane multiply-and-rotate hash, D210) told two reads of a staged file apart; `Seen`/`Seeing` (`crates/core/src/store.rs`, length plus the format's CRC-64 on `crc-fast`, D223, D233) told two reads of an upload's bytes apart. D210 kept its own hash because the table CRC was slow; D210's "revisit if" named exactly the change D223 then made. Should the token use `Seen`?
- **Decision:** yes. `Witnessed` keeps a `Seen`; the sync and upload paths feed a `Seeing`. `ByteTally` and its constants are deleted, and `lsr_core::ByteTally` with them. This also follows the owner's ruling that common primitives come from established crates (D302).
- **Measured:** 64 MiB of fresh random bytes per process, hashed 16 times, in the engine's 1 MiB read chunks: `Seeing` against `ByteTally` -61.8% time [-62.6%, -61.0%], 20 interleaved pairs, p<0.001, A/A ±0.9% (n=12), SESOI 5%; Apple M2 Max, rustc 1.100, release profile, default features. At 4 KiB chunks -63.2% [-64.2%, -62.1%] (12 pairs). Under about 64-byte chunks `crc-fast`'s per-call cost makes it slower (+69% at 37-byte chunks, 10 pairs), but no caller adds chunks that small: the encoder's output and the store's reads are both whole buffers. One machine; not repeated on x86-64, where `crc-fast` uses the same carry-less multiply.
- **Cost accepted:** none found. The token stays 16 bytes. The CRC-64 detects every burst of 64 bits or fewer, which the lane hash did not promise.
- **Revisit if:** a caller starts feeding the check in pieces of a few bytes, or a platform without carry-less multiply becomes a target.
