## D182. The soak reaches its rarest marks from short explicit scenarios, not from luck

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-fable-5-2), delegated · Recorded by: Claude (claude-fable-5-2)

- **Question:** the nightly soak fails its coverage gate with no failing scenario, because some required marks (an app action armed right before one of the engine's rarer calls into SQLite, and an injected failure of the write lock) are reached 0 to 3 times per 1,000 seeds, and the soak runs about 1,600. D173 removed engine callers of some of those calls, which lowered the rates further.
- **Choice:** each such mark gets one short explicit history in `crates/sim/src/scenario/coverage.rs` that puts the engine in the situation that makes the call and arms the action right before it. The soak runs them once ahead of its seeds and adds only the keys each names to its reach. A scenario that stops reaching its key fails the soak and `marks::the_coverage_scenarios_reach_their_required_marks`. The gate demands what it demanded before.
- **Measured (6,000 seeds from 6000000, 500 each from twelve bases, the nightly's laws):** `point.BeginWriteLock.Commit.ran` 0, `point.PageCount.DeleteWal.ran` 0, `cell.source.BeginWriteLock.DiskFull.hit` 5, `point.ReadPage.ReaderBegin.ran` 16, `point.ReadPage.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` 4, `point.ReadPage.Checkpoint.Restart.ran` 3, `point.Checkpoint.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` 2, `point.BumpSeq.ReaderEnd.ran` 5, `point.BumpSeq.Checkpoint.Restart.ran` 11, `point.BeginBarrier.ReaderEnd.ran` 9, `point.EndBarrier.ReaderEnd.ran` 7. All are reachable: the scenarios reach them.
- **Exemptions kept:** the five exempt marks that a soak reaches now and then (`cell.source.EndWriteLock.Io.hit` 3, `point.PageCount.Commit.ran` 1, `point.PageCount.ReaderEnd.ran` 0, `point.PageCount.Checkpoint.Truncate.ran` 0, `point.EndBarrier.Checkpoint.Passive.ran` 6, of 6,000) are too rare to require without a scenario each; their reasons now cite these counts.
- **Why:** raising the weight of the app's in-tick action would raise every point's rate by the same factor and still leave the rarest at luck; a scenario is exact and costs milliseconds.
- **Cost accepted:** a mark a scenario reaches is no longer evidence that random histories reach it, and a change that makes the random draw unable to reach it passes the gate. The rates above are the record of what random draws reach.
- **Revisit if:** the soak's seed count is restored to the tens of thousands, when most of these rates support requiring them from the seeds alone.
- **Links:** D173; `crates/sim/src/marks.rs`.
