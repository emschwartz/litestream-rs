## D296. Each swarm-reachable plant carries an unaided budget or a stated miss (delegated)

Status: decided · Decided by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), **delegated**; the owner can overturn it · Recorded by: Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5) · test and tooling only (`scripts/planted-bugs.py`, the justfile, the nightly workflow, the entry files)

- **Question:** `just planted-bugs-unaided` printed seeds to the first failure per plant and judged nothing, so a plant the swarm stopped finding went unnoticed. What must a run judge?
- **Choice:** every non-canary entry of `lsr-sim` and `lsr-core` (254 entries, 219 distinct patches) carries `unaided = <seeds>` (the swarm must fail within that many seeds from base 1,000,001) or `unaided_miss = "<why>"`. `just planted-bugs-names` (in `just check`) refuses an entry with neither, with both, or whose entries that share a patch disagree. `--unaided` fails on a budgeted plant found later or never, and on a marked miss the swarm finds within `--seeds` (default 100): the miss is stale and its sentence is false. A plant whose first failure is the clean tree's own is reported as masked and fails too. `just planted-bugs-judge-test` (in `just check`) runs the judge over fake rows.
- **Budget rule, fixed before the judging run:** budget = min(300, max(4 x measured, 50)), where measured is the seeds to first failure in the one measuring run below. The cap is that run's seed count: a plant first found past seed 75 gets 300, which is headroom of under 4x.
- **Measuring run:** one run of `--unaided` with 300 seeds per plant from base 1,000,001 at 4918e715e (the swarm at the time of this change; generator unchanged by it, one registration). Of 219 patches, 46 failed within 300 seeds (63 entries) and 173 did not (191 entries). Measured seeds to first failure, sorted: 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 4, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6, 7, 8, 8, 13, 17, 17, 18, 18, 18, 21, 26, 29, 29, 29, 30, 33, 44, 63, 73, 76, 79, 86, 151, 200, 206, 231, 249, 258, 258, 263. 19 plants get the floor of 50 and 11 get the cap of 300. Each entry file carries its own budget.
- **Judging run:** the same deterministic base (1,000,001). The swarm is deterministic per seed, so this is a regression gate (it fails when a swarm change, a generator change or a patch drift moves a first failure past its budget), not a fresh estimate of recall. A fresh base would measure recall again but with 4x headroom the budgets would flap on seeds the measuring run never saw; recall on a second base is a separate registration. A miss is judged on the first 100 seeds only: the deterministic 300-seed run found nothing in them, so a failure there means the swarm changed.
- **Misses:** the 191 entries marked `unaided_miss` carry one sentence each, in ten sentences over seven classes (patches per sentence: 13, 31, 49, 7, 26, 20, 23, 2, 1, 1). The sentence is a classification read from each entry's own comment and its named tests, not a trace of why 300 seeds pass: the stale-miss check is what keeps it from drifting the other way, and a sentence proved wrong by a trace is rewritten, not the budget. The classes are: fail-stop lag that only a named fail-stop scenario holds long enough; cost plants (requests, memory, bytes) that change no restored page; compaction or listing state that needs a backlog or a hole; the local course; single decision points needing one precise fault; plants in the simulator's own judges; and interleavings at one request or tick.
- **Cost, measured:** one 200-seed run of one plant took 153 s wall (180 s user) on a loaded 12-core machine with its build; 500 seeds of the clean tree took 290 s (0.58 s a seed under load average 14); the whole measuring run took about 3.6 hours of wall time (three runners at once, one restart for the chunk change below) on a machine at load 14 to 55. Budgeted plants run their budget (6,162 seeds in total), misses run 100 (17,300), and each shard runs the clean tree once for up to 300: about 26,000 seeds and 219 builds, an estimated 30 minutes a shard in eight shards (about 240 runner-minutes a night, ubuntu-latest). The nightly job `planted-bugs-unaided` has a 60-minute limit; the first nightly calibrates the estimate. Misses at 300 seeds would have cost 52,000 seeds and about 70 minutes a shard, over the limit.
- **Also changed:** the swarm is run 25 seeds per nextest invocation (was 100): at 100 seeds a loaded box passed the 90 s per-test limit, which showed as `ERROR` with no first-failure line (24 of 219 plants in the measuring run).
- **Cost accepted:** a budget with 4x headroom detects a swarm that got much worse, not one that got somewhat worse; a swarm change that moves a first failure from 6 to 40 passes. The miss sentences are hypotheses until a generator change tests them.
- **Revisit if:** the generator changes (re-measure and rewrite every budget in one commit), a shard nears 40 minutes, or the stale-miss check fires for a plant (set its budget from that run).
